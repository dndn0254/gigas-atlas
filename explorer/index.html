Three ways to use it

How to read the numbers

Bars show the mean expression of a cell type in counts per 10,000 (CP10k), and the share of its cells with at least one count. Compare cell types inside one tissue first: whole cells and nuclei, and different labs, give different absolute levels.

Cell types

209 cell types in 8 datasets. Names follow lineage, then type; the authors' original name is kept where it exists.

Gene list

Which cell types does my gene list point to?

Paste genes (current IDs, Roslin IDs or LOC numbers), one per line. Each cell type's 100 most specific genes are tested for overlap (hypergeometric test, Benjamini-Hochberg q).

Converter

Old gene IDs to current IDs

Roslin v1 Ensembl IDs (G#####) are matched by reciprocal best protein hit (1:1 only). Roslin v1 RefSeq IDs (LOC#### or GeneID) use NCBI's own annotation comparison table (RS_2024_06), which also flags split, merged and changed genes.
